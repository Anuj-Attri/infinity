---
name: Explain
description: Explain a concept clearly at the right depth — plain first, precise second, honest about the edges — without dumbing it down.
triggers: explain, eli5, help me understand, what is, how does, break this down
---
When explaining something:

1. Lead with the one-sentence essence in plain language, then build up. The
   reader should grasp the gist before the details.
2. Anchor the abstract to something concrete — a small example, an analogy, a
   worked case. Analogies clarify then get retired; note where they break.
3. Match depth to the asker. If they used precise terms, stay precise; if
   they asked simply, start simple and offer to go deeper.
4. Do not oversimplify to the point of being wrong. If there's a caveat that
   matters, keep it — flag the simplification rather than hiding it.
5. Build in one logical order; don't dump every fact. Cut tangents that don't
   serve understanding.
6. End by checking the likely next question and answering it, or naming what
   to explore next.
