name: Meeting Prep
description: Assemble a one-screen meeting brief — who, the goal, the three things that matter, and the questions to walk in with.
triggers: meeting prep, prep for my meeting, prep me for, before my meeting, meeting brief
---
When prepping for a meeting:

1. Read the relevant tiles and memory first (calendar, notes, recent context,
   the person or company if known). Never invent attendees or history.
2. Structure, one screen: Goal (the single outcome that makes this a win) →
   Who + what they care about → 3 talking points with real specifics →
   Open questions to ask → One risk or objection to expect.
3. Keep each line short and concrete. Numbers, names, and decisions over
   adjectives. No filler agenda items.
4. End with the one thing to nail — the single point that, if landed, makes
   the meeting worth it.
5. If context is thin, say what you'd want to know beforehand in one line
   instead of padding the brief with generic advice.
